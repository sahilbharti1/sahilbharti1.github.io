I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering, School of Engineering, at Chanakya University, Bengaluru. My research focuses on robot-assisted sheet metal forming (roboforming), plasticity and phenomenological ductile damage modeling, finite element simulation, and industrial automation for aerospace and automotive applications.

I earned my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras (defended December 2024), where my doctoral work examined toolpath strategies, heat-assisted forming, geometric accuracy, and fracture forming limits in incremental sheet forming. During my doctoral tenure, I helped set up an experimental robot-assisted forming facility supported by a Rs. 137.5 lakh Department of Heavy Industry and Mahindra & Mahindra (DCAAI) grant, and co-authored 6 peer-reviewed journal articles (including *Nature Scientific Reports*) alongside 3 patent applications. I also hold an M.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Gandhinagar and a B.E. in Production Technology from Madras Institute of Technology, Anna University.

At Chanakya University, I am building the Chanakya Advanced Robotics & Mechatronics (CHARM) Lab and guiding research under the Genesis Protocol. I also teach B.Tech courses in Engineering Mathematics (Probability and Statistics), Engineering Mechanics, Robotics and Automation Systems, and Physics for Engineers.
